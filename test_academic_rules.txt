COLLEGE ACADEMIC RULES AND REGULATIONS - 2026

1. ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENTS
Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in all courses to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examinations.
Students with attendance between 65% and 74% may request a condonation on medical grounds with proper documentation.
Any student with attendance below 65% will be automatically barred from examinations.

2. GRADING SYSTEM
The college uses a 10-point scale for CGPA calculation:
- Grade A+ (10 Points): Excellent (>= 90%)
- Grade A (9 Points): Very Good (80% - 89%)
- Grade B (8 Points): Good (70% - 79%)
- Grade C (6 Points): Average (50% - 69%)
- Grade F (0 Points): Fail (< 50%)

3. HOSTEL REGULATIONS
Hostel gates will close strictly at 9:30 PM. Late entry requires written permission from the Warden.
A security deposit of 5000 INR is required at the time of hostel admission. It is fully refundable upon graduation.

4. TUITION AND LIBRARY FEES
Syllabus booklets and library access cards are provided free of cost.
An annual library maintenance fee of 1500 INR is charged with the semester tuition.
